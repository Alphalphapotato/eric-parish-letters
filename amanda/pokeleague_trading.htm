Trading

Trading makes your pokémon smarter and stronger so try to trade offen.
But when you trade you don't have to trade a pokémon you like.
When you trade sometimes the pokémon won't obey you or the other
trainer. Trading is more then just giving another pokémon to a person
and the other trainer giving a pokémon to you. First you should battle the
two pokémon that you and another trainer will trade. Then look for a trading
machine. Now check the other pokémon's stats if it is a higher LV. then you can handle don't trade.
