What are Pokemon?
 

Pokemon is short for Pocket Monsters.
Pokemon first get captured then trained. When pokemon
aren't doing stuff they go in a ball called a pokeball. But some species of
pokemon dislike going in pokeballs like Pikachu and Meowth.
There are more then 100 species of pokemon. Pokemon are
divide into 15 types of pokemon which includes electric, fire, water,
flying, psychic, ghost, rock, ground, dragon, grass, ice, normal, fighting,
poison, and bug. Pokemon can fight, each type of pokemon has
different attacks like Magmar a fire-type can learn Ember, Fire spin,
Fire blast, and Flamtrower.
