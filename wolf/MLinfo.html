General Info

This is a wolf rp, and I want it to be a real-like wolf rp. There will be no
wings, no super high jumping, blah blah blah. But some skills will be like
good hunter, kinda faster, blah ble blah. The only thing not real-likish (other
than talking) is creativity! We are able to make.... maybe drawings, bone
knifes, rafts. Other than that, REALISTIC.

Hunting Info

The hunting will be real lifish too. I will play the animals once you try
to hunt something. We will hunt in teams, but you can hunt by yourself.
If you hunt by yourself, you'll probably only be able to catch a rodent or
rabbit. If you hunt in the team, we can catch deer, blah, and if lucky,
moose! Sence this is realistic, we will have to eat. If you don't, you won't
die, just may get sick or unable to fight.

Puppy Playing Info

We gotta play to learn! Puppies seem to play a lot. Only puppies
can play, but adults can train and talk. There are a couple of games
that puppies play. There is the most common, Chase or Tag. It
is the same game. There is an it. The IT puppy runs around trying
to get the other puppies to make them it. Hint when playing this
game puppies; don't just run to catch the other puppies, hide and
jump! Another game is wrestle. It is very simple. The two puppies
wrestle each other and roll over each other. You can also make
up little puppy games also!

Mating and Birth Info

All males and females can mate and give birth, but the alphas
seem to have the most puppies when birthing. Go to Mating
Lake to find a mate, and Birth Cave to have birth. When
mating, two males or two females can fight over a mate, but
please try to avoid this. When birthing, I say what the pups
turn out to be like and how many. You name the pup and I
say what it looks like and the personallity. The puppy may
be slightly like the mother and father.

Battle Info

You can battle for one of 3 things. For a mate, for a
rank, and for skill. When fighting for a rank, only
males can fight males and only females can fight
females. When fighting for skill, you can fight
anybody. Your skill will be shown on the hunting
board. The more skill you have, the better you get
at hunting and fighting. If you lose a fight, you just
get injured.

Sickness and Injured Info

During the end of the wet season, it is more common
to get sick. When sick, you can't hunt nearly as good.
Just wait and eat good for the sickness to go away.
Also if you don't eat for a while you may get sick.
If you get injured badly in a fight against another
wolf or hunting, you have to care for it. Try not to
move around to much and care for the injured spot.
You can care for the spot by mostly putting water
on it.
