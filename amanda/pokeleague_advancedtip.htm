Advanced Tips

Here are advanced tips for you who won at least 4 badges
 

1. Train your pokémon evenly, you don't want a pokémon on LV. 30
and another on LV. 5.

2. Now you should start trading some pokémon they will then get smarter
and stronger.

3. Make friends with other trainers so your pokémon can battle once in
a while. ( pokémon really like battling )

4. Let your pokémon have fun or let them spend some time in Pokémon
Paradise. It will help you and your pokémon.
