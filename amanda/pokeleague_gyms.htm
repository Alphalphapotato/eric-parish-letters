Gyms
 

Gyms are pokémon battles that are harder then the other battles. When you
beat the gym leaders you get pokémon badges and if you get all 8
pokémon badges you can enter the Elite Four and then battle me and get
the pokégods.
 

Here are the weaknesses and resistances of the gym leaders.
 

Brock - Whip him with plant pokémon or drown him with water pokémon.
But just don't use normal or electric pokémon because Brock uses rock-type
pokémon

Misty - Shock her with electric pokémon or drain her with plant pokémon.
Don't use rock or ground pokémon because Misty uses water pokémon.

Lt. Surge - Dig him with ground type pokémon but if you put water or flying
type pokémon you'll get zaped.

Erika - Burn her with fire type pokémon or bug them with bug type pokémon.
Don't use rock or ground  because Erika uses plant pokémon.

Sabrina - Use bug or rock pokémon to win against Sadrina. Just don't use any
flying or fighting pokémon.

Koga - Use psychic pokémon or use ground pokémon. Don't use fighting
or grass type pokémon.

Blane - Put out his flame with water or rock. But he'll burn you if you use
plant or bug pokémon.
