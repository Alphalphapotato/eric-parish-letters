North County Blade-Tribune May 2, 1989
Remembering Abbie
Editor:
 I'd like to take a moment to say goodbye to Abbie Hoffman, one of my teachers from the 60's.
 Since everyone these days seems to be rewriting Vietnam, Abbie's death made me sit down and remember. It made me remember napalm and My Lai, all that good old "peace with honor" stuff.
Let's not do this again in Central America.
 Rest in peace, Abbie.
ERIC PARISH
Vista

North County Blade-Tribune August 10, 1989
Racism is prevalent
Editor:
 I don't quite understand Mr. R. L. Secinaro's "Community Forum" article on Aug. 2.
 Is he saying that the Blade-Citizen has a leftist bias when printing about "Illigal Aliens, Mexican-Americans, and Mexico?"
 I don't really care what "Legal" ethnic group Mr. Secinaro comes from, but racism is very prevalent in North County, San Diego. Ask Mr. Metzger in Fallbrook.
 Ask the two legal aliens gunned down by the teen-ager off Black Mountain Road.
 I see those lime-green border patrol vans every day these days. We all have a right to live in this desert. Especially the people who pick the food. Give us a break.
ERIC PARISH
Vista

North County Blade-Tribune September 4,1989
U.S. not good guys?
Editor:
 Racial taunts in San Diego? "All Mexicans are whores!"? The United States Border Patrol screams obscenities at the impoverished third world (Aug. 29)?
 Is the guilt from seeing real poverty being understood by the "born-to-shop crowd?"
 Does our country really condone shooting 15-year-olds for throwing a rock? This happened two hours after the Border Patrol agent had worked his crowd up to a proper frenzy.
 I am deeply embarrassed by our country's official policy on these matters.
 Subjugation of a people, as in El Salvador, isn't even economic good sense anymore, as we can read in the paper, except for the rich, of course;  they just transfer their wealth to our country.
 Are we the good guys anymore, or what?
ERIC PARISH
Vista

North County Blade-Tribune October 18, 1989
Pity pro-lifer
Editor:
 I feel some slight pity for poor Dena Niehouse (Oct. 12 Blade-Citizen), the teen-ager who did 10 days for trespassing at the La Mesa abortion clinic in lieu of a $300 fine.
 It did seem to be a rather long article for a simple criminal trespass case. I agree she must feel strongly that abortion is murder. However, a lot of us simply believe it is a woman's right to choose whether or not she wants to bear children. And we believe that very strongly too.
 Most "radical" right-to-lifers will tell you they don't even believe in birth control. Just total abstinence or 13 kids.
 Personally, I think there are some people who just don't like sex in general. It's too messy and complicated, and sinful to boot.
 As we say, "If men got pregnant, abortion would be a god-given right." And we all know he is male.
ERIC PARISH
Vista

North County Blade-Tribune November 17, 1989
Viewpoint shocks
Editor:
 I can't believe the Viewpoint interviews you printed on Sunday, Nov. 12. You asked the question "Should the United States have entirely open borders?" Since East Germany just opened their borders, it was a good question.
 All six of your respondents said no, with various degrees of hatred toward ethnic groups. You interviewed one black man, two white men, and three white women. Where were the Mexican-Americans? I couldn't believe the quote by Ms. Rejah Kahaan:"The United States doesn't belong to the United States anymore."
 Try to get a little more balance next time.
ERIC PARISH
Vista

North County Blade-Tribune December 28, 1989
Bush bearing gifts
Editor:
 I guess I got my Christmas present from George Bush today.
 It's day 2 in Panama and the count is 21 dead. I was watching Mr. Bush's press conference, with the stiff neck and laughing and Santa Claus handing out presents.
 At the same time, the planes were unloading the bodies of our servicemen.
 Not to mention the trampling of Panama.
 Merry Christmas, George. Have a nice day. Ho, Ho, Ho.
ERIC PARISH
Vista
