North County Blade-Tribune February 14, 1988
Packard 'drivel'
Editor:
 I wrote a letter to Congressman Ron Packard about his support for the so-called "freedom fighters" in Nicaragua.
 I got back a two-page form letter mentioning the phrase "freedom fighter" no less than 10 times. He didn't mention "Somoza" once.
 He never mentioned that the good ole USA has been invading Central America since before Ortega, Castro, the Soviet Union, or even communism was around.
 As a closer to his letter, he couldn't resist saying "... many liberals have been duped by this communist propaganda."
 It's the old "commie dupe" syndrome again.
 I guess anything Ron says is truth delivered from on high.
 I stongly recommend everyone write this guy themselves, and read his drivel for yourself.
 Does our district really deserve another two years of this man's "leadership"? I don't think so.
ERIC PARISH
Vista

North County Blade-Tribune March 3, 1988
Editor:
 More and more these days I hear about all the poor Vietnam vets.
 Poor, poor guys. Thrown into that war thinking it was a piece of cake.
 Maybe we should have a Vietnam Vet month? I've actually seen an "official prisoner of war" license plate.
 I think it's funny you never hear about that 10-1 kill ratio. Are you listening? That's at least a half-million dead Vietnamese. The real total is much higher. Kind of like Israel vs. Palestine.
 We got off easy and hopefully learned our lesson on being the boss of the world.
 You people can'y just rewrite history. Some of us remember Vietnam.
 ERIC PARISH
Vista
