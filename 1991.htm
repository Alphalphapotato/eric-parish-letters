Oceanside Blade-Citizen March 11, 1991
Ashamed of U.S
I am amazed at the pro-war rallies all over Oceanside. Iraq suffered 200,000 dead and we lost 94. It sounds like the new world order to me. I will never fly my U.S. flag again.
We should all be really ashamed of ourselves as a nation. This was worse than Vietnam.
ERIC PARISH
Vista

Oceanside Blade-Citizen March 25, 1991
Fight not about freedom
Editor:
 I would like to reply to all the people who think I should move to Iraq. (Blade-Citizen, March 15).
 I'm not quite sure how making thousands of Iraqi widows is "fighting for my freedom" to write a letter to a newspaper. I was similarly confused 20 years ago when we made millions dead Vietnamese to "protect my freedoms." Restoring the emir and his 24 wives might be "fighting for my freedom" to buy cheap gas, but that's about it. I almost forgot our freedom to spend billions on weapons of war.
 So thanks a lot, President Bush.
ERIC PARISH
Vista

Oceanside Blade-Citizen May 3, 1991
Refuses to celebrate
Editor:
 It's almost too ridiculous to believe.
 Thousands of flag fanatics in Oceanside welcome home a bunch of soldiers who actually traveled halfway around the world and back in billion-dollar machinery.
 Meanwhile, back in the Mideast, Iraq has been without electricity for months; Kuwait is still under the same dictatorship, but on fire now; and a million Kurds are refugees. Israel is still "borrowing" land from the West Bank. Everyone hates everyone. Peace has been set back in the region for years.
 I'm not going to celebrate. Not at all.
ERIC PARISH
Vista

Oceanside Blade-Citizen July 4, 1991
Nothing slow about growth
Editor:
 To be honest, I think all this slow-growth talk about North County is a joke. Ask anyone who has been here for the last 20 years what has happened to our bucolic paradise. Traffic, people, traffic and more malls. Ask all those poor people in Orange County who have lived there for the last 40 years.
 I'm still waiting for "Rancho Pendleton" myself. Too bad, we live in such a nice part of the world.
ERIC PARISH
Vista
Escondido Times Advocate  August 22, 1991
Education, not abstinence
 The problem with Dr. Sinsky's answer to teen-age sex (Letters, Aug. 20), chastity and morality, is that Dr. Sinsky is no longer a teen-ager.
 Although I am over 40 now, I remember being a teen-ager. All I thought of then was sex. People who recommend chastity to modern teen-agers are stupid, ignorant, or both. There is nothing at all wrong with the sex act itself. Pregnancy and disease can be prevented by education, not abstinence. It's too bad we live in a culture that regards sex as somehow innately immoral, and thus end up thinking about it all the time.
 Provide the education and the condoms. Keep our normal,healthy and sexually aware teen-agers happy.
                                                                                       Eric Parish
                                                                                                 Vista
