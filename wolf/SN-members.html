Classes:
 

Warrior: Warriors are very good at claw to claw combat. They
are very good but some don't use they're brains. Level 1 warriors
can jump 2 feet higher than a normal wolf. They can also move
and attack slightly faster. Level 2 warriors can jump 3 feet
higher than a normal wolf and can still move and attack slightly
faster. Also level 2 warriors can go into a small rage. Level 3
warriors can go into a slightly bigger rage and can attack faster.
They can still jump 3 feet higher but they can make their claws extend
2 inches bigger. At the final level 4, Warriors can jump 4 feet higher. They
can also move alot faster and attack alot faster. They can go into a big
rage and are very strong. Their claws can extend 3 inches bigger.

Sorcerer/Sorceress: Sorcerers/Sorceresses are wolves that
uses elements for fighting. Wolves that are not Sorcerers
or Sorceresses can use elements, but are not as good as they
are with the powers. Most Sorcerers/Sorceresses use 1 of
3 main elements for their main. These 3 elements are the
element of Fire, the element of Water, and the element of
Nature. Level 1 Sorcerers can use their main element slightly
better than they would. Level 2 Sorcerers can fly for a short
while and can use their main element still yet better. Level 3
Sorcerers can use their main element even better and can
fly for a long while. Also level 3 Sorcerers can shoot
beams of their main elements. At the final level, Level 4,
the Sorcerers can fly until they get tired and have mastered
their main element. They can shoot missles of their main
element and are very dangerous for the monsters.

Spy: Spys are wolves that are mainly used to get info
from many different monsters and wolves. They are
very agile and hard to see when hiding. Level 1 Spys
know exactly where the best place to hide is. Level
2 Spys can be invisable for 40 seconds. Level 3 Spys
can be invisable for 120 seconds and can make good
hiding places. Level 4 Spys can become invisable for
5 minutes and can make very good hiding places. Also
level 4 Spys can read other animal's minds, though they
can not change them.

Summoner: Summoners can summon different kinds of
animals and make them help you fight. Summoners usually
have some kind of magical item that the animals can be
summoned to. Level 1 Summoners can call 1 raven, 1
bunny, and 1 rat. Level 2 Summoners can call 2 ravens,
1 dark bird, 2 rabbits, and 1 snake. Level 3 Summoners
can call 3 ravens, 2 dark birds, 1 wild dog, and 2 snakes.
Level 4 Summoners can call anything in a half a mile
radius! (for that last one, just tell me you want to do that
and I will tell you the animals in the circle o.O)

Paladin: Paladin wolves can make Auras that gives him
special abilites for a short while. When I say the Levels,
the wolf can do the Aura in it's level and above. Level 1
Paladins can make an Aura that will alow him to be
slightly stronger for 2 minutes. Level 2 Paladins can make
an Aura that can let him fly for 2 minutes. Level 3 Paladins
can make an Aura that can make them go twice as fast for
3 minutes. Level 4 Paladins can make an Aura that allows
the wolf to look into and change another animal's mind
for 1 minute.
