Oceanside North County Times  February 21, 1997
Lies, deceit got the U.S. into Vietnam
Editor:
 It has been 25 years since the end of the Vietnam debacle and I still don't understand some people's thinking about that war (Mary Jane Skarda, Letters, Feb. 14).
 After Robert McNamara's memoirs you would think it was common knowledge about all the lies and deceit it took to get us into that war and keep us there. But some people still blame the protesters for the death and destruction visited upon countless families. I think they have it backwards. If we had not ended the war in 1972, who knows how many more would have died? And it doesn't take a mind reader to see that slaughtering a few more million Vietnamese wouldn't have made the world any safer for democracy. Jane Fonda may have been a trifle tactless- she admits as much in one of her books, but she had the right idea. As for Tom Hayden, I don't recall him ever going to Vietnam during the war. He was one of the founders of the Students for a Democratic Society and one of the "Chicago Seven," but I do not believe he was ever in Vietnam proper. Soon he may add "mayor of Los Angeles" to the list. I wish him luck.
                                                                                         ERIC PARISH
                                                                                                      Vista

Oceanside North County Times  July 3,1997
Gays just human
Editor:
 I applaud your June 23 editorial pointing out the intolerance of the Southern Babtist Convention for boycotting the Disney Studios.
 In the Letters section, however, I noticed another gay-bashing letter by one of your tired,old, Bible-thumping readers. Then a thought occured to me: Your editors are most likely young and somewhat cosmopolitan. In other words, they probably know some gay people on a social level. On the other hand, I would hazard a guess that most of the letter writers who assume God Almighty has stricken down the Sodomites with the AIDS virus have never in their lives met a normal gay man or woman. So it starts to make a bit more sense to me.
 I guess these homophobes just don't know any gay people. Otherwise, they might notice that they are human just like the rest of us.
                                                                              ERIC PARISH
                                                                                            Vista
Oceanside North County Times  July 24, 1997
Life too glorious for creationism
Editor:
 You printed a letter on July 14 from a gentleman who implied that anyone with a Darwin fish on his or her car is somehow a racist. This is simply not true.
 Some groups in the past have tried to use "social Darwinism" to imply some races are more evolved than others, but that has nothing to do with what Darwin believed or the science of evolution.
 People have always tried to explain their bigotry with science or religion. In fact, because the Bible is older, it has been used to justify racism far more often than Darwinism. The story of Cain and Abel comes to mind.
 The Darwin fish is nothing more than a simple reminder that not all of us believe in an 8,000-year-old earth, with all of life essentially created at once as it appears today. Life is too mysterious and glorious to have such a sterile history.
                                                                               ERIC PARISH
                                                                                            Vista

Oceanside North County Times  August 29, 1997
The 'sheep' have shown their mettle
Editor:
 It is fitting that Chris Pulse wrote his last letter about the bleating of sheep (Aug. 24). If anyone sounds like a whiner, it is Mr. Pulse. It has been a few years now that he has been trying to get others to agree to his opinions on race, the Vista school board, and his other rancid little right-wing ideas.
 As we "sheep" have shown our mettle by electing Bill Clinton and getting government to show a little compassion for those who have trouble helping themselves in this lifetime, Mr. Pulse's remarks have grown more and more strident. Lately, his letters have been nothing more than name-calling. In fact, he sometimes sounds like another great American patriot of the far right, Timothy McVeigh.
                                                                                  ERIC PARISH

Oceanside North County Times  December 1, 1997
Right wing trips over itself with grave "scandal"
Editor:
 I have had fun watching all the Clinton bashers jump all over themselves about this new "scandal" they have "unearthed". Supposedly, burial spaces in Arlington National Cemetery were "sold" to major Democratic Party donors.
 Great stuff for the right wing. Drive military veterans into screaming hysterics,etc. Only one problem however: It is not true. Not one speck of it. Of course truth hasn't ever bothered these people much, so the story, just by being in the paper, now seems to be taken as gospel by people who don't like Clinton anyway.
 A few of the major newspapers have already editorialized their apologies and printed the truth of this "scandal". I would like to see the North County Times do the same. I kind of doubt it, however. How would Thornhill figure out how to apologize for last Sunday's cartoon?
 The poor man has enough trouble thinking up ideas to inflame people. I don't think Mr. Thornhill has ever cared much about the truth anyway.
                                                                                ERIC PARISH
                                                                                              Vista
