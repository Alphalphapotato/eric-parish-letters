Eating/Hunting: Sure, this is like an RPG. But also
it is like a regular Wolf RP. Wolves gotta eat! Plus,
some foods give some wolves extra powers, strength,
etc. Puppies (age 0 months to 1 year) have to eat quite
a bit. Pups have to eat at least 2 times a month or they
shall die. Puppys can not hunt for themselves, they need
to be given food. Teenagers (age 1 year to 3 years) have to eat
1 time a month. Teenagers can hunt!! Even though
they have not mastered hunting, they can! Adults (age 3
years to 8 years) are good at hunting. They have to eat
1 time every 2 months. Elders (age 9 to 12 years) are getting
older and are not as good as hunting. They have to eat 1 time
a month because of their getting older.

Aging/Dieing: Dieing can happen in 4 ways: They
can be beaten by a monster, severly damaged in a
wolf fight, not getting enough food in the time
needed (see Eating/Hunting), or getting to old.
Once you die, you must go to either the Graveyard,
Ghost Vally, or make a new charicter and
forget the dead one. For aging, it is pretty
simple. One real day is a month in the
site. So 12 days is a year. It is as simple as
that.

Mating/Repoducing: When two wolf

one is Gemmed Cliff. You try to find some gems
and that will grant you.. urr... stuff. When battling,
