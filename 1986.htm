North County Blade-Tribune December 4, 1986
Two is two too much
 I can't take it anymore! Who is W.G. Wilson, anyway?
 Usually you publish one of his right-wing fantasy diatribes only once or twice a week, so I open the Sunday paper (Nov. 30) - and there are two pieces on the opinion page, one in the letters and another in "Community Forum." My community is Vista, too.
 I quote: "The worst mistakes President REagan has made was his approval of the genocide treaty and his approval of sanctions against South Africa."
 Who is this world is for genocide or apartheid?
 The genocide treaty makes a lot of far-right people upset because it acknowledges the Holocaust as real. For some reason, there are people who don't believe Hitler was so bad, just because he didn't like Gypsies or Communists either.
 As for South Africa, their situation is totally indefensible. People are people and justice is justice.
 I don't know who your connections are at the Blade-Tribune, but please - no more double opinions.
 Two is two too many for a Sunday morning, a day of peace.
ERIC PARISH
Vista

North County Blade-Citizen December 8, 1986
Thinly-disguised racism
Editor:
 Enough is enough!
 Who is W.G. Wilson anyway! He seems to have South Africa on his mind these days.
Mr. Wilson, how would you like to live as a black or Mexican-American-Chicano in our town of Vista today?
 I'm sure you've seen the Border Patrol vans at the Broadway bus station or on South Santa Fe Drive so I know you could understand the reality of pass laws, walls around Soweto and nine-year-old boys in prison.
 Your thinly-disguised racism makes very poor reading. I don't know your connections, but I'd as soon not see any of your old-man right-wing drivel in the only community newspaper we have in North County.
 I would also like to thank Michael Dean Miller (Dec. 1) for pointing out that when it's said and done, satanists have the same rights as do all of us. How many cows are sacrificed for one Burger King!
ERIC PARISH
Vista

North County Blade-Tribune December 26, 1986
Don't forget Bastille Day
Editor:
 So, Alice Johnson likes W.G. Wilson's "double-barreled shotgun of truths" (Dec 19). I guess if the paper printed 10 letters by Mr. Wilson in one issue, you could call it an "MX missile full of truths."
 Since Mr. Wilson's letter in the Dec. 19th edition was incoherent and incomprehensible, I will reply to Ms. Johnson.
 The "culprits of the United Nation" you talk about who forced the genocide treaty on us happen to be the rest of the world. Believe it or not, the United States is not the whole world and nobody likes genocide.
 And since when is South Africa's inexcusable policy of apartheid  a "border problem"? Lily-white South Africa has black people overrunning it's borders looking for jobs? Ha! Ha! Ha!
 I realize its real easy to hide out in North County with other expatriate southern rednecks and pretend that your swimming pool fantasy is real and the real-life oppression and poverty of the Third World is all caused by "communists".
 But even if you go to your graves believing in Ronnie and the Marines, your kids and grandkids are going to have to pay the piper for the massive arming of this world for war. The idea of Karma is ancient, you reap what you sow.
 Although you call me a "socialist, college-taught liberal," I've never been to college. It occurs to me that maybe a higher education has passed you by, too. Maybe I'm just not as old as you.
 And that wishing me a "Merry Lenin Day"?
 The poor and oppressed of the world have been celebrating May 1 since 1917.
 Merry May Day to us all, each and every one. Don't forget Bastille Day.
ERIC PARISH
Vista
