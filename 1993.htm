Escondido Times Advocate    January 4,1993
Creationists, in a word
 I can hardly wait for the return to school here in Vista. I'm referring to the story (Dec 27) about our new school trustees and the teaching of creationism.
 I picked up a textbook at a local thrift store called "Biology for Christian Schools." It comes complete with a diagram of Noah's ark, detailing exactly how all those animals got along for a whole year. This is an actual school textbook.
 I was laughing and crying at the same time.
 You quoted Joyce Lee, one of our new trustees, as saying that, by teaching only about evolution, schools send a message that Christian parents are stupid.
 I'm afraid I have to agree with you there, Ms. Lee. I would use a more polite word like misinformed, however.
                                                                                   Eric Parish
                                                                                              Vista

Oceanside Blade-Citizen   March 18,1993
Killer no Christian
Editor:
 I guess it was just a matter of time before the anti-abortion fanatics decided to start shooting doctors. These people somehow think of themselves as "Christian." They must be followers of this new "Christ" from Waco,Texas.
 I apologize to all the true and sincere Christians who disagree with the right of women to reproductive choice. It is a serious difference of opinion, and in a free society, both sides must have their say. But when people like Randall Terry, director of Operation Rescue, can excuse the murder of a doctor because he was a "mass murderer," it is time for true Christians to take a long, hard look at what they believe- and at the people who are acting as their leaders.
                                                                               ERIC PARISH
                                                                                              Vista

Oceanside Blade-Citizen   April 16, 1993
Pity for Metzger
Editor:
 So, Tom Metzger, you think North County has "a Negro and a Mexican problem?" (Letters,April 8).
 I've always thought it ironic that an avowed white racist would choose to live in Fallbrook, Southern California. Look around, Mr. Metzger. Vista, Escondido, San Diego, Los Angeles - these are all Spanish names. We live in a part of the world conquered by Mexico and then by the United States.
 Do you really feel that you are superior to someone because you were born with white skin, and theirs is brown or black? It is a shame you've squandered your life supporting racial hatred. I feel a tinge of pity, but not much.
                                                                                ERIC PARISH
                                                                                              Vista

Oceanside Blade-Citizen   June1,1993
Fighting racism
Editor:
 I'm writing in response to H. Anthony Barker's ridiculous letter on racism (May 13).
 Racism is defined as racial prejudice or discrimination.
 Believe it or not, Mr. Barker, but it is pretty hard for black and Hispanic people to discriminate against the majority race in this country.
 Particularly I am sickened by your use of the word "turnskins" to denote someone with white skin who works against the appalling racism lingering in our society. I grew up in Texas in the 1950s and am very aware of what racism is. So consider me a "turnskin" and a "betrayer of one's own people." I can't believe the Blade-Citizen printed rubbish like your letter.
                                                                                 ERIC PARISH
                                                                                               Vista

Oceanside Blade-Citizen   July 17,1993
An ironic story
Editor:
 That was a very amusing news story about Rep. Ron Packard "lambasting" environmentalists (July 9). I congratulate the writer, John Lamontagne, for his delicious sense of irony.
 First off, we read that Democratic congressmen are "in the pocket" of the environmental lobby. This is spoken by Packard as he is addressing a breakfast meeting of the Building Industry Association at the Lomas Santa Fe Country Club in Solana Beach.
 So in which lobby's pocket do you reside, Mr. Packard? I am also "very discouraged about the direction this country is taking with the environment."
 You should think more of what kind of world your grandkids will grow up in, and less of how fast we can turn North County into another Orange County. Think about it, Rep. Packard.
                                                                                   ERIC PARISH
                                                                                                 Vista

Oceanside Blade-Citizen   August 26,1993
Keep creationism out
Editor:
 So the Vista school board wants to teach "creationism" in the social studies classes and not in the science department.
 I remember the last election when the so-called "Christian Right" said it had no "secret agenda" to impose on all of us nonbelievers. Seems we were all wrong.
 It's not enough that Vista Unified School District trustess John Tyndall, Deidre Holliday, and Joyce Lee have made our town the laughingstock of the country, not to mention the legal fees.
They actually believe that life began in a Garden of Eden, and that Eve is only Adam's rib.
 Not all of us believe in fairy tales, and we don't want it taught to our children in public schools.
 Religion is best taught in those strange buildings with crosses on the roof that seem to be only occupied on Sunday mornings.
 Public schools, on the other hand, are for everyone, whatever race, creed, etc.; and they are open five days a week.
 I'm not sure a recall is the best way to go, but I would sure like to see people interested in educating our children running in the next election. You will have my vote.
                                                                                        ERIC PARISH
                                                                                                     Vista
