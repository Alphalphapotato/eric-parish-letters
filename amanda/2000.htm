North County Times  January 8, 2000
Editor:
 I see that Mark Thornhill is still very confused (Jan 5). Comparing cigarette filters to condoms is rather bizarre. I think most of us agree that no one wants to see anyone start smoking cigarettes. They will kill you. On the other hand, I think most of us parents want our children to have sex someday, and lots of it. I want many grandchildren myself.
 The problem seems to be that some parents think their children should be married first before having sex. This seems to be some kind of a religious belief as far as I can tell. Sexually transmitted diseases are rather scary but have been around for ages. Condoms have been proved to stop them if used appropriately. If religiously inclined people wish their children to go to the marriage bed intact, that is OK by me. However, trying to impose their pseudo-morality on everyone else using the public school system is not OK.
 I think all these "no-sex-before-marriage" people should answer a couple of questions the next time they try to impose their morals on the rest of us.
This goes for  Mr. Thornhill, too. Were you and your spouse both virgins on your wedding night? And if so, why do you think you should make everyone else be?
                                                                         ERIC PARISH
                                                                                 Vista
