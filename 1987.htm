North County Blade-Tribune January 1, 1987
Who are you kidding?
Editor:
 In reply to W. G. Wilson (Dec 23):
 Your ridiculous ideas on South Africa irritate me no end.
 This is an African nation we're talking about. They were highly civilized a long time before anybody thought of circling a wagon.
 You seem to have this white-man's-burden mentality, Mr. Wilson. Considering the generosity the white race has bestowed on Africa, a small wonder the trouble is only beginning.
 Do you have any idea how much good could come from the war machine money being spent now in Angola and South Africa? Thousands of babies could be nurtured, homes could be built, crops could grow.
 But no, we must fight the dreaded "Communist." Who are you kidding? A Communist is a homeless, hungry person, no more, no less.
 We're talking about the obscene distribution of wealth, and the rip-off imperialism that goes with it.
 Like I was trying to tell your friend, Alice Johnson, the sooner you dinosaurs go your own way, the sooner we can get this world back on track.
ERIC PARISH
Vista

North County Blade-Tribune January 11, 1987

Editor:
 Who is Charley Reese?
 I guess it's sort of interesting that Charley is scared to be mugged.
 However, you cannot in any way compare a back alley mugging to international relations. It is millions of people trying very hard to get along in a world of limited everything - except people.
 If you want to blow away some muggers, Charley, go ahead. If you actually think we can just blow away all the communists, or whoever else you feel threatened by, forget it. This is 1987, not 1941.
 You can't just kill all the Nicaraguans, Russkies, etc., Charley. It's not civilized and you couldn't do it anyway, Charley. Remember Vietnam?
I think one of our great strengths as a nation has been our realization of the horror of nuclear weapons. I guess we could have "saved" Vietnam if we had turned it into a nuclear desert, but we didn't.
 Who do you want to save nect?
 I quote, "men and iron can take bread and money."
 If that's not fascism I don't know what is. That kind of mugger mentality has to be pointed out whenever it lifts it's slimy head. Sure sounds like Hitler talking to me.
 It seems to me that humanity has honestly tried lately to be civilized with itself.
 Your kind of divisive hatred may be typical of some parts of North County, but it has no place in the international arena.
 So kill all your muggers, Charley, just leave the rest of us alone.
