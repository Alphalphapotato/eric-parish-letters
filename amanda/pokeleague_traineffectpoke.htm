Trainer's Effect With
Pokémon

When pokémon battle the pokémon's trainer effects how the pokémon battles.
Here are the ways you will effect how your pokémon attack.
 

Get along with your pokémon - This makes a HUGE diffrent in how your pokémon battle.

Give your pokémon a vacation once in a while - This will higher your pokémon's attack, speed, and obedience.

Train your pokémon - This will higher every rating exept obedience.

Make friends with your pokémon - This again makes a HUGE diffrent
in how your pokémon attack.
