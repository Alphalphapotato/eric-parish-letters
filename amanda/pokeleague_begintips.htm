Beginner Tips
 

If your a beginner and your pokémon won't obey you or your pokémon are weak this page will help you and your pokémon.
 

1. Make friends with your pokémon, then they will obey you and get stronger.

2. Battle your pokémon when you have the chance to and when your pokémon
get hurt heal them at a pokémon center.

3. Trade your pokémon they will then get smarter and they will level up quicker.
