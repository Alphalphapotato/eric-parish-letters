Hunting Rules
  • Our hunting is like wolves, plus we gotta eat!

Battling Rules

Social Rules
  • Obey the ranks above you. Everyone Obeys me (Kai).

Eating Rules

  • Childs have to eat at least 3 times a week.
  • Teenagers have to eat at least 2 times a week.
  • Adults have to eat at least 1 time a week.
  • Elders have to eat at least 3 times a week.
Age Rules

  • Elders need some help to do stuff. But they are very wise.
  • Every day you get one year older. (I may have to make up because I cannot be on everyday)
