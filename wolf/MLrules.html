Rules

Hello all you new peoples! Welcome to the MoonLight Wolf Pack. These
are the rules. If you break any rules, you can get a warning, be banned for
a week, or be banned from the pack.

General Rules

  •  No power playing
  • No godmoding
  • No porny stuff
  • No cursing anybody
  • Once you have read all these rules, on the joining form, where it says Rule Words, put 'respect da alpha'
  • The most important rule: HAVE FUN!

Hunting Rules
  • I'm the animals, don't control the animals
  • Wolves don't always win the hunt!
  • be realistic! don't say 'wolf1 sneaks up on the deer and clamps it's neck, killing it'
  • There are no elements, but you can use your traits well!
  • Lead hunters can start a hunt, but others can't. The others can just hunt by themselfs unless allowed to join in the hunt.
  • Don't even TRY to take down an adult moose by yourself, you'll just get hurt.
  • Preditors like bears may interupt hunts, so look out.
  • No gorny stuff, don't say blood sprays everywhere or anything like that.
