Oceanside Blade-Citizen March 16, 1990
Times have changed
Editor:
 I see you have that fossil W.G. Wilson writing letters to the editor again (March 12).
 I like when he calls the "one-man, one-vote" system of our government "socialism, pure and simple, as Karl Marx advocated."
 Maybe it should be "one corporation, one vote." Or even "one dollar, one vote."
 Or, like they have in South Africa, "one white man, one vote?"
 As usual, Mr. Wilson's letter didn't make any sense whatsoever. They do start me laughing, however.
 Hang in there, Mr. Wilson.
 Some day we might all live in 1925 again. Heaven forbid.
ERIC PARISH
Vista

Oceanside Blade-Citizen June 1, 1990
On recent police shootings
Editor:
 Congratulations to Vista Sheriff's Deputy John S. Wickham for blowing away an unarmed, unaware 21-year-old carpenter in the driveway of his own apartment complex.
 Too bad that Jeff's pregnant wife wasn't looking out the window. Her husband was left dead and handcuffed, face down, for five hours.
 Another of our brave officers blew away the transient on Interstate 5 - the one armed with the deadly cement trowel.
 Maybe someone could inform these "Dirty Harrys" that most of us are law-abiding citizens trying to earn a living.
 Do these guys watch too much TV in their off hours? Miami Vice,etc?
 Personally, I think it's the feel of that big,hot hunk of metal nect to their leg that turns a cop into "Mr. Fast Draw."
 Deputy Wickham should be thrown in prison. He'll probably be back on the job next month. With a month of paid leave under his belt.
ERIC PARISH
Vista

San Diego Tribune July 12, 1990
Iran has reasons
 What's wrong with all these people who've been writing letters to your paper protesting American earthquake aid to Iran? (7-2-90).
 Did they forget how our CIA put the shah in power years ago? Never heard of the shah's dreaded secret police? Armed with America's best war toys, of course.
 By the way, people, our country is the No. 1 arms dealer in the world. Does that make all you superpatriots feel really proud?
ERIC PARISH
Vista

Oceanside Blade-Citizen   August 24,  1990
Who came first?
Editor:
 Do any of your anti-immigrant readers realize that the piece of land we live on was Mexico for a long, long time?
 It is so funny to read all the tirades against illegal aliens in your paper. People don't realize that the West was actually colonized by an East Coast-oriented United States, or they try not to think of it too clearly.
 Today California, tomorrow Saudi Arabia.
                                                                               ERIC PARISH

Oceanside Blade-Citizen September 9, 1990
On anti-Mandela letters
Editor:
 Has anyone else noticed this avalanche of anti-Mandela letters to the Blade-Citizen? They all seem to come from Vista, too.
 The letters all remark on the African National Congress's admittedly communist affiliation, and the violence that is always present in a country ruled by a white minority intent on remaining in power.
 Someone has organized a letter-writing campaign to the Blade-Citizen and is trying to turn back the progress being made for democracy in Africa. It is so obvious that I'm curious why the Blade-Citizen keeps printing all these letters, sometimes more than one a day.
 Who encouraged all you Vistans to suddenly write letters to the editor against one of the truly heroic figures of our century?
ERIC PARISH
Vista

Oceanside Blade-Citizen   November 12, 1990
Deja vu
Editor:
 I sure live in a strange country. We can never admit we are wrong. Remember Vietnam? We seem to still have trouble remembering who had right on their side in that war. Now we seem to have transported another 200,000 soldiers half way around the world to protect our interests?
 Does any other nation on Earth get away with this kind of arrogance? Last I recall, our borders were the Atlantic and Pacific oceans. Now I understand what the military buildup of the 1980s was all about. Let's all enter that long, dark tunnel again
                                                                                    ERIC PARISH
                                                                                                 Vista
