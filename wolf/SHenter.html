Below is the story of the ShadowNight Wolves.

In the middle of the ring of fire, (called the Eye of Fire in this RP,)
there lies a small island. This island had a good balance of good and
evil. Yet one day, the leader of the good went across the seas and never
came back. The last ray of hope got defeated and evil was all that was
left. Chaos and trouble filled the island as unimaginable monsters
evovled and grew. This is a time of confusion and evil.
